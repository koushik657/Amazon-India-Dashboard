# Amazon-India-Dashboard
Build an interactive Amazon India Dashboard to analyze sales, profit, products, orders, customer order status, payment methods, fulfillment, and geographic performance. Students will transform business data into clear KPIs, charts, and visual insights to help management understand overall performance and support better business decision-making.

1) Overall Sales & Profit Performance
Business Question:
How is the business performing overall?
Dashboard should show:
Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Orders
Total Units Sold
Average Order Value
Profit Margin %
Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
Expected Insight:
Overall Sales & Profit Performance.
2) Category & Product Performance
Business Question:
Which products and categories are driving the business?
Dashboard should show:
Category-wise Sales
Category-wise Profit
Category-wise Units Sold
Top 10 Products by Sales.
3) Order Status & Revenue Loss
Business Question:
How many orders are successfully completed and how many are being lost?
Dashboard should show:
Delivered Orders
Shipped Orders
Returned Orders
Cancelled Orders
Return Rate %
Cancellation Rate %
Category-wise Returns & Cancellations.
4) Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance
Business Question:
Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance
Which payment methods, fulfillment methods and states are generating business?
Dashboard should show:
Sales by Payment Method
Profit by Payment Method
Sales by Fulfillment Method
Profit by Fulfillment Method
State-wise Sales
State-wise Profit
State-wise Orders
